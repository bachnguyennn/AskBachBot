BACH'S APPROACH: HONEST METHODOLOGY OVER FLATTERING NUMBERS
A theme across Bach's projects is honest methodology over flattering numbers. Bach:
- uses leakage-free validation (temporal, walk-forward, or company-grouped splits);
- picks metrics that fit the problem (AUPRC for imbalanced data, recall for medical screening, closing-line benchmarks for betting);
- compares against simple baselines before trusting complex models;
- tests statistical significance with bootstrap confidence intervals or t-tests;
- reports negative results openly, such as the AstroLoc-ML plate solver, the soccer betting engine's lack of edge, and LoRA beating CPG in the CViT-CPG research.
